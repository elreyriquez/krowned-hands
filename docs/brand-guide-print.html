Brand style guide

Mobile massage and bodywork in Kingston and Montego Bay. Use this guide so every touchpoint feels like the same calm, premium practice.

Who we are

Positioning. Transformational body work: pain relief, deep recovery, and nervous system reset, delivered in the client’s space.

Personality. Warm, grounded, confident, clear. Premium without feeling cold or corporate.

Imagery. Favor visuals that feel relaxed, peaceful, calm, and happy—warm light, ease in the body, and quiet joy rather than stiff or clinical scenes.

Logo

Use the approved mark and wordmark together or the mark alone where space is tight. Keep clear space around the lockup at least equal to the height of the crown in the mark. Do not stretch, recolor, or add effects to the logo files.

Photography & imagery

Use natural light, tactile detail, and real environments when they support the story. Portraits and treatment shots should read as relaxed, peaceful, calm, and happy—never rushed, cold, or overly posed. Rounded corners and a light frame match the site.

Dark accents and counter balance

Let warm cream carry most of the story, then use charcoal as a deliberate counter balance for emphasis: testimonials, pricing, booking, and key CTAs. Pair charcoal with cream type and gold accents so the dark reads premium, not flat. A very soft, blurred gold glow behind content (low opacity) keeps the accent warm rather than loud.
